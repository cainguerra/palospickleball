About Palos Youth Pickleball

Our Mission

Palos Youth Pickleball is a non-profit organization dedicated to building community through the sport of pickleball. We are youth teaching youth—passionate young players sharing their love of the game with beginners in the Palos Heights, Palos Hills, Palos Park, and surrounding communities.

What We Do

We organize casual meet-ups and informal lessons where youth players can learn pickleball from their peers. No pressure, no complicated schedules—just young people coming together to play, learn, and connect through the sport.

  • Informal Lessons: Casual instruction from youth players
  • Meet-ups: Opportunities to play and connect with other kids
  • Peer Learning: Kids teaching kids in a relaxed setting
  • Community Connection: Making friends through shared play

Why Pickleball?

Pickleball is one of the fastest-growing sports in America because it's accessible, social, and fun. It's the perfect sport for bringing people together and building community. Our youth instructors love sharing this game because:

  • It's easy to learn and quickly rewarding
  • It's social and promotes teamwork
  • It's great exercise and builds healthy habits
  • It creates lasting friendships

Our Story

We're just getting started, but our vision is clear: use pickleball as a way to connect young people in our community. Our youth instructors are passionate players who want to share what they've learned and help others discover this amazing sport. As a non-profit, we're driven by community service, not profit—our goal is simply to get more kids playing pickleball and building connections that last.
